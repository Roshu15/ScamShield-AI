STAY ONE STEP AHEAD

Essential safety tips

🔐

Never share OTPs

No legitimate service will ask for your one-time password.

▣

Never share UPI PIN

You only need a UPI PIN to send money, never to receive it.

↗

Verify suspicious links

Check the sender and website before opening any link.

◷

Don't panic under pressure

Scammers create urgency. Pause before you respond.

☎

Use official support

Use details from the official website.

↓

Don't install unknown apps

Only download apps from trusted stores.

ScamShield AI provides awareness and guidance. Always verify suspicious transactions or claims through official channels.
